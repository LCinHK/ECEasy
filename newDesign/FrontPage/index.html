Quick FAQ

Common questions for demo viewers, supervisors, and new users.

Is this production-ready?

This page is a demo front page. The chatbot backend is local-first and configurable for testing workflows.

Can I switch knowledge sources?

Yes. The backend supports switching between ECE FAISS knowledge and legacy Chroma knowledge by configuration.

Where should I start?

Use the launch button to enter the chatbot, then try a course-code question such as COMP2011 or ELEC2400.
